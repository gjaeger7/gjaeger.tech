Plan your visit

First time at an Orthodox church?

Orthodox worship can feel unfamiliar at first — icons, candles, chanting, incense, standing, and ancient prayers. You do not need to know what to do before you come. You can simply be present, listen, and ask questions afterward.

Children are welcome

Our faith

The Orthodox Church is ancient Christianity, alive today.

For many people in Central Nebraska, Orthodoxy may be unfamiliar. A good parish website should offer a simple, welcoming starting point: not overwhelming, not watered down, but clear enough that a curious visitor knows what to do next.

Serving beyond Kearney

A regional parish for Central Nebraska seekers.

Because Orthodox parishes can be far apart, the website should make it clear that people from surrounding communities are not outsiders. St. George can be presented as a spiritual home for Kearney, Hastings, Holdrege, Grand Island, Lexington, and the rural roads in between.
