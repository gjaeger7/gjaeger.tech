Plan your visit

You do not need to know everything before you walk in.

Visiting an Orthodox church for the first time can feel intimidating. That is normal. You may see icons, candles, incense, chanting, standing, crossing, and worship that feels older and deeper than what you are used to.

You are welcome to simply come, watch, listen, and pray as you are able. No one expects you to know the service already.

What is Orthodox Christianity?

The faith of the apostles, lived today.

Orthodox Christianity is the ancient Christian faith preserved in worship, teaching, sacrament, prayer, and community. For many visitors, the first step is not reading a long theological explanation — it is being invited into a living worshipping community.

Scripture, hymns, icons, incense, prayer, standing, reverence, and a sense that worship is something we enter into — not something we watch from a distance.
