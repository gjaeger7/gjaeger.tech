How this board is built

Simple, useful, not pretending to be MLS.

Listings are pulled from Zillow-indexed public results because Zillow blocks direct automated fetching. Owner and prior sale details come from public county records where available. If the latest transfer shows $0, the board skips backward to the most recent non-$0 sale amount.
